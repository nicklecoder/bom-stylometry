Rate comparisons control genre the same way on both sides and show the effect of each control step. Counts come from the source language where one exists (the Hebrew, not the KJV's English). Units that don't carry across languages (words) are checked against one that does (verbs). A rate mismatch counts as evidence only against a comparison text matched in genre and date; with no matched text the result is inconclusive. Constitution II.
