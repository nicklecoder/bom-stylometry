Zeniff's first-person record (Mosiah 9–10, embedded kind "narration") counts as narrative frame, not embedded speech. It is a first-person account of events (migration, war, kingship), the same kind of text as Nephi's first-person account in 1 Nephi, which is frame; speech, letters and prophecies set inside the narrative are what the frame step removes. PREREG 2 registered it as frame. The earlier published figures counted it as speech; bom-buckets reports the frame figures that way as a sensitivity check.
