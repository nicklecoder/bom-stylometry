Reading the earlier, unpublished project's four-measure placement battery (narrative chaining, coordination, 'behold', body-part prepositions; not reproduced in this repo) as evidence about source language. Rejected for that battery: on two of its measures the book's own internal spread exceeds the whole Hebrew–Egyptian distance, so any placement depends on which books are averaged, and in its scale-free rebuild every English text, novels included, landed nearer Hebrew. This rejection does not cover the question this repo tests: whether the book's formula uses split into a Hebrew-style use (setting-first) and an Egyptian-style use (straight to the event). That is measured by the bucket comparisons, the additive model and PREREG 2 (results/wayhi/egyptian, results/wayhi/prereg-event-share); the rate test is inconclusive, and PREREG 2 tested only a match to the Egyptian particles alone.
