Counting past wayhi and future wehayah together (Parry-style, unpointed ויהי + והיה = 1,224, near the 1,204 often cited) as the Hebrew equivalent of 'it came to pass'. Rejected: it pools two tenses and their look-alikes; the Book of Mormon's past 'came to pass' is compared with past wayhi only.
