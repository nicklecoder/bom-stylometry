An Egyptian rate counts for or against the book only if the text matches in genre (connected narrative) and date (composed about 600–500 BC or earlier; older is acceptable). Demotic sentences and pRylands 9 fail on genre; their rows are reported but cannot decide. Matched narratives span 0–22 per 1,000, bracketing the book, so the rate test is inconclusive.
