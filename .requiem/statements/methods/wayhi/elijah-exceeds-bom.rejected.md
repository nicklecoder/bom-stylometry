The claim that the Book of Mormon uses the formula 1.6× as often as Hebrew and that the Elijah cycle exceeds it. Rejected: it counted ordinary-verb wayhi. On setting-first uses the book is 2.7× the 17 narrative units, and no single unit (Elijah highest at 5.81 per 1,000) reaches its 7.76.
