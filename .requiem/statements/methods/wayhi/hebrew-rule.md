Hebrew wayhi/wehayah are bucketed from OpenScriptures morphology by what follows: setting-first (a time word, a preposition + time noun, a circumstantial participle clause, a preposition-led clause), straight-to-event, or ordinary verb (wayhi with its own subject, 'and there was light'). Only setting-first counts as the narrative formula; Hebrew has no straight-to-event wayhi. The KJV renders setting-first wayhi as 'came to pass' 84% of the time.
